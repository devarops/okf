title: Utilization Distribution Overlap Index (UDOI)
description: An unbounded index for quantifying shared space use between two distributions.

The Utilization Distribution Overlap Index (UDOI) quantifies shared space use between two spatial probability distributions.
The continuous form multiplies the overlap area by the integral of the product of the two utilization densities.
Over a discrete grid with uniform cells, UDOI is the overlap area times the sum of the cell-wise products.
When each distribution is normalised to sum to one, the cell area cancels.
UDOI then equals the number of overlap cells times the sum of the cell products.
The index is not bounded to the unit interval.
Values greater than one indicate overlap concentrated in a smaller area than a uniform distribution would produce.
UDOI should be computed on conditional 95% utilization distributions to exclude negligible tail probabilities.
Use UDOI as a relative ranking metric for comparing overlap across distribution pairs.
It is not an absolute probability and should not be interpreted as one.
The index works for any pair of normalised spatial distributions, not only home ranges.
Estimates of UDOI are biased; the bias depends on sample size and the true degree of overlap.
